Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

Who handles your data

The data controller is Manuel Cárdenas, of Los Vicentinos 270, Los Ángeles, Biobío Region, Chile. For anything about your data: masdemanu@gmail.com.

The most important thing, first

Pronuncia uses the speech recognition built into Google Chrome. That component belongs to the browser, not to us, and it works by sending your microphone audio to Google's servers to be transcribed. The same happens on any website that uses Chrome's dictation.

We never receive, listen to or store your audio. But we could not offer the Service without that component, and we think you should know before you subscribe rather than after. Google's handling of that data is governed by their privacy policy.

What data we do NOT handle

  • Your audio. It never reaches us.
  • The transcripts. Comparing what you said against what you were meant to read happens inside your browser. Nothing is sent or stored.
  • The text you practise on, or the pages you visit. The extension only reads the text you select, and processes it in your browser.
  • Your card details. You enter those into Paddle's form. They never pass through our systems and we have no access to them.

We use no analytics, no trackers and no advertising cookies. We do not sell or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train models.

Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract: your account, your licence and your payments. Without this data we cannot provide the Service.
  • Legal obligation: keeping accounting and tax records.
  • Consent: microphone access, which you grant in the browser and can withdraw at any time from Chrome's settings.

If you use a "remote" voice for the reference audio, the text of the word you click is sent to that voice's provider to be synthesised. The extension prefers local voices from your system whenever they exist, partly for this reason.

These providers process data outside Chile. Where the data of buyers in the European Union or the United Kingdom is involved, those transfers rely on adequacy decisions or on standard contractual clauses, as the case may be.

How long we keep it

Your account data, for as long as your account is open. If you close it, we delete the data within 30 days, except billing records, which we keep for as long as Chilean tax obligations require — the tax authority may review returns for up to six years.

Your rights

You can ask us to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Write to masdemanu@gmail.com and we will respond within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the authority in your own country of residence — in the European Union and the United Kingdom, your national data protection authority. In Chile, personal data is governed by Law 19.628 and, from 1 December 2026, by Law 21.719, which creates the Agencia de Protección de Datos Personales as the supervisory authority.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16. If we find an account belonging to someone under that age without valid consent, we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the date above and tell you at your account email before the changes take effect.
